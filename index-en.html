    Hizio – AI job search agent

    Product AI Job search Automation Side project

    Description

    Hizio is an AI job-search agent I first built for myself to find a First PM role. I then turned it into a SaaS product after realising there was no similar solution on the European market.

    The idea is simple: instead of spending hours scrolling job boards, Hizio centralises more than 150 job sources (APEC, HelloWork, LinkedIn, Welcome to the Jungle, etc.) and only alerts you when a role is genuinely relevant to your profile.

    The agent analyses your CV, criteria and goals, builds a rich profile (far beyond a simple job title), scores both the offers and your candidacy, and surfaces concrete levers you can act on to maximise your chances.
